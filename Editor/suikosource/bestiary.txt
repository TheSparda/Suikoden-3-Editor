Suikoden III: Guides: Bestiary
The bestiary is a listing of where all of the enemies in the game can be found, their HP, and their dropped items.
SuikodenIII: Guides: Bestiary by Kuromimi
Area and Treasure Bosses

Area Boss :- These are enemies that are encountered very rarely in certain areas. The amount of experience, potch and skill points acquired from area bosses are usually much higher than what is normally available in the current area. Some area bosses also drop very rare items.

Treasure Boss :-These are enemies that appear at the very end of a dungeon, guarding a treasure chest. These treasure chests usually contain excellent items if opened as soon as possible. Treasure bosses respawn every fourty to sixty minutes, provided all the items from the chest have been removed.

Story Bosses :- These can be found in a seperate file here.
