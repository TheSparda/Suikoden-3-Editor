I.) Introduction
The purpose of this FAQ is to list the 108 Stars of Destiny 
of Suikoden III, explain how they are recruited, then, provide 
detailed information about these stars, and explain how effective, 
or ineffective, they may be. Following this, a list of storyline 
boss battles is provided, as well as effective teams and 
strategies to help defeat these bosses. 

II.) 108 Stars

The following is a list of all 108 Stars of Destiny. All stars will 
follow this format

Name: The name of the star

Automatic: Detailing whether or not the star is gotten
automatically or not. If they aren't, then this will
explain how to obtain them. Not all optional characters do 
not have to be gotten in the chapter that they are listed, but
it may be wise to do so, as you may not be able to reach them
for a while, or they have a certain level advantage that proves
handy. To recruit non-automatic stars, you must visit Budehec 
Castle first. Geddoe can reach it in Chapter 1, Chris chapter 2,
and Hugo will automatically go there in his Chapter 3. Thomas 
can start recruiting after he has his "land-lease" idea.  

Battle Fighting Style: Some characters fight differently
then others. Pure magic users will rarely attack, and
berserkers will almost always attack, even if issued a defend
command. Here is the list of styles:

       Offensive Fighter: Will attack if you say to, defend if
you say to, and if the other person in the pair uses magic or an item, they 
will attack.
       Defensive Fighter: Will attack if you say to, defend if
you say to, if the other person in the pair uses magic, they will 
defend, and if the other person in the pair uses an item, they will attack.
       Berserker: Will attack if you say too, will attack if 
you issue a defend command, and if the other person in the 
pair uses magic or an item, they will attack
       Magic User: Will defend if you issue an attack command 
(EXCEPTION: if there are two magic users in a pair, the one in
the front will attack, and the other will defend), defend if 
you say to, and if the other person in the pair uses magic or 
an item, they will defend.
       Support: Is a support character, does not fight.


War Fighting Styles: What the character does in strategy battles.
The choices are:
     Leader – They lead a unit. They must fight in wars where
they are the leaders, and when they die, the unit is defeated. 
     Fighter – They fight in units led by one of the leaders, and 
may be switched around from unit to unit unless it is expressly stated in
the walkthrough that they cannot. These characters do not have to fight
unless they cannot be moved.
     Support – They offer support skills, but do not fight in the 
battle themselves.
     Doesn’t fight – They do not fight in war.
     Mixed – They fulfill more then one role over the course of 
the game. The rating will be broken down by which role they fulfill 
if the rating differs.
 

Skills: Unique skills for each character. These skills are skills 
such as Heavy Damage that not everyone learns. 

Strengths: Highlights what the character does best in a situation. This will
also detail important skills to raise to get the most out of a character. A
Support character will not get this category

Weaknesses: This will detail the character's drawbacks, and ways to counter 
them, if they can be countered. A Support character will not get this 
category

Support Discussion: This applies to Support characters. This section will
discuss how useful the support abilities of a character are. Also, if 
applicable, this section will explain what the character does at Budehec 
Castle and where to find them. Certain playable characters, like Juan, will
have this section if they perform a function at Budehec Castle. 


Skill List: A list displaying the maximum levels each character’s 
skills can reach

Stats - Like all games, characters have stats that factor in what they
can do better then others. The following list are the stats 
of the Suikoden III world.

PWR - Power. Physical, offensive power. 
SKL - Skill, Determines into accuracy
MAG - Magic. Determines amount of spells one may cast before resting
REP - Repel. Determines dodge
MDEF - Magical Defense, determines how well one fares against magic. 
SPD - Speed, factors into how fast a character can move
LUCK - Luck, helps determine random variables
HP - HP, helps determine how durable a character is. 

Physical defense is determined by the armor a character wears, with the 
exception of animal stars, as they cannot equip any armor. The stats in 
this game are a "base" of sorts. They factor initial ability, but one 
trains up skills (physical skills at a Bujitsu Teacher, magical skills 
at a Tutor) to help make the characters better. Some skills are trained 
better then others, evident by the grades that can be reached. 
	

Geddoe Chapter 1

Name: Geddoe
Automatic: Yes
Battle Fighting Style: Defensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Leader 
Unique Skills: Freeze, Parry, Magic Repel

Geddoe is one of the three main characters that can be played as in 
Suikoden III. He is a mercenary, the captain of the 12th unit in the 
Harmonian Southern Frontier Defense Force. He’s a mysterious character; 
not even his partners know that much about him. In truth, he is the 
bearer of the True Lightning Rune, and one of the oldest people in the 
world. He fought alongside the Fire Bringer against Harmonia fifty years ago. 
He wears black clothes and chainmail, and he uses a broad sword to fight.

Strengths: Geddoe will always bear some type of True Rune, so he'll have a great
magical offensive ability. Couple that with his above average magic stat and 
high magical skills, he will dish out magical offense with the best of them. 
Geddoe is also tough enough to handle himself, with a great Parry and alright
Repel and Armor Protect so that he will usually not need to be protected with a
tank, and his magical defense is fine too, with a high MDEF and Magic Resistance
to supplement his only average Magic Repel. 

Weaknesses: Geddoe, perhaps not surprisingly, has a low SKL rating, so he'll
tend to miss. To compensate, Geddoe can usually just attack with magic, or train
up his Accuracy to help a little.

Name: Ace
Automatic: Yes, he starts with Geddoe
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter 
War Fighting Style: Fighter
Unique Skills: Discount, Thief

Ace is the accountant of the 12th unit in the Southern Frontier Defense Force, 
a job he handles because no one else wanted it. He loves to drink and chase 
skirts. He used to be a member of Gilbert’s mercenary crew (Gilbert from 
Suikoden II). He’s got brown hair, wears a brown and beige patched shirt with 
a red scarf tied around the arm, camo pants, and he uses a pair of sai to 
fight. He learned about sai from a book and had a blacksmith make a pair.

Strengths: Although most people don't try it out to realize it, Ace is actually
a very efficent damage dealer, with the ability to boost his attack and hit in
an area with 100% accuracy. The sacrifice is that the rune will unbalance him, 
although building up Ace to his highest potential will more often then not 
kill the enemies before the second round. Ace can also hold his own in most 
situations, with a high REP stat to avoid attack, and above average HP to help 
his above-average defense and average magical defense. Another good thing about
Ace is that he requires very little maintenance, he doesn't need a rare equip
like a Fury Rune. 

Weaknesses: Ace is very balanced in the sense that he really has no weaknesses.
He's got a low MGC stat, but Ace does more damage with physical attacks anyway,
so that's pretty useless, and even if you want Ace to use magic, he uses 
Lightning, so he'll outdamage anyone who uses an Earth, Wind, or Water Rune. 
Perhaps the only other real drawback is that Ace only has 4 free skill slots,
so he can't do absolutely everything, although he's not the kind who needs 
many skills to be effective anyway.

Name: Queen
Automatic: Yes, she starts with Geddoe
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Fighter
Unique Skills: Heavy Damage, Shield Magic

Queen is a member of the 12th unit in the Southern Frontier Defense Force,
and she is the only woman in the unit. Queen is a tomboy; she enjoys drinking 
as much as the men in the unit. She used to be an aristocrat in a country 
called Sanady, but Harmonia conquered it and she was forced to live as a slave 
as a child. She got black hair, wears a green shirt and black pants, and she 
uses a one handed saber to fight. 

Rating
Strengths - Queen's very high on offense, with a quick weapon for multiple 
swings and Heavy Damage to dish out pain, and with her Repel, she can avoid
most hits. Queen's also got a high MGC stat and will be perhaps the best Wind
Magic user until the end of the game when Yuiri becomes available. Thirdly,
Queen gets the skills she really needs as natural skills, so she doesn't need
to worry much about her skill slots, since she only needs one free slot (for 
Damage) 

Weaknesses - Perhaps Queen's only weakness is her lack of a second free rune 
slot. She gets one, but her Ripple rune is only useful for scoring a 100% 
accurate hit since it lessens damage. As such, Queen can only use one of her 
strengths, magic or physics. She can dual-class with more efficency then 
Percival since she outdamages him. Her lower HP can raise problems, but as a 
female, she can equip a Blue Ribbon.

Name: Joker
Automatic: Yes, he starts with Geddoe. 
Battle Fighting Style: Defensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Support
Unique Skills: Counter-attack, Continual Attack, Focus, Pale Gate Magic

Joker is the oldest member of the 12th unit in the Southern Frontier Defense 
Force. He’s foul-mouthed and enjoys making cracks at Ace. However, Ace and 
Joker get along surprisingly well, and the two of them, along with Queen, 
enjoy their drinks. He’s got black hair and a mustache, wears all purple, and 
a bunch of tribal necklaces, and he uses his fists as a weapon. For those who 
have played Suikogaiden, Joker is Wan.

Strengths: Joker is one of the best mages you'll have. Although he doesn't 
possess a True Rune, he has the highest magic stat in the game, which makes 
for the mostspells. As such, Joker has powerful magical abilities, with the 
exception of Lightning. He makes for an excellent dual-wielder of Fire and 
Water. Joker is also one of the best fighters among the magicians, with 
Continual Attack that most fighters would kill for. Also, among wizards, Joker 
is easily the most survivable, with an A in Repel, the highest HP, and even a 
B+ in Armor Protect. So essentially, Joker has the best non-mage talents of a 
mage, fighting for those times when spells would be not worth using, and 
defense since, well, everyone can use defense. But his magical abilities are 
still top-notch

Weaknesses: As a wizard, Joker's survival abilities, although the best among
the wizards, are at best, average. Joker's other drawback is that his Isshin
Rune is not that useful, and his third slot opens much later in the game. 
Joker does not possess a natural S in magic, but it's very easy to reach an S 
in Fire with easily found equipment.

Name: Jacques
Automatic: Yes, he comes with Geddoe
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter 
War Fighting Style: Fighter
Unique Skills: Sharpshoot

Jacques is the newest member of the 12th unit in the Southern Frontier Defense
Force. He’s really quiet, and doesn’t drink alcohol like the rest of the 
group. He’s rather strange; no one knows exactly why he does what he does. 
He’s blonde, wears a light blue coat and khaki pants, as well as a white 
headscarf. He uses a gigantic crossbow to attack.

Strengths: Jacques has the best Sharpshoot, which grants him some critical 
ability and allows him to attack at any time without moving. He's also decently
tough and doesn't have to worry about dying most of the time, especially 
against monsters like Chimeras that target archers. His Boranda Hawk rune is 
also very good at sweeping the field of enemies since it hits everyone and does
a higher damage multiplier then Great Hawk, and that it's much, much faster. 

Weaknesses: Jacques's damage, although fine for blitzing commons, really tapers
off when he goes against single strong enemies. Although he can reach two shots
with a C swing, he'll be outdamaged by Geddoe, Ace, and Queen all the time. 
He's also unsuitable for magic, but he's better with a Double-Strike Rune to 
use in conjunction with Boranda Hawk anyway.

Name: Aila
Automatic: Yes. In Karaya Village, Ace and Joker will see her argue with a 
woman and go chase her. Pursue them, and when Karaya is attacked, Aila will 
join with Ace and Joker. After you meet Geddoe and Queen again, Aila will join 
permanently. This is all automatic. 
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Doesn’t fight
Unique Skills: Shield Magic, Freeze

Aila is a young Karayan girl who travels with Geddoe. She has some ability to 
sense the spirits, but she tends to stick to her bow rather then her wits. 
She hates the Zexen Knights for their attacking and destruction of Karaya 
Village. She wears bright yellow clothing, has brown hair tied in curls, 
and carries a short bow to attack. 

Strengths: Aila is very good in the early game. Since Geddoe's brigade lacks 
a dedicated healer, she easily fulfills this role with her Shield Rune, and can
do this even until the late game. She's can do some decent far-away damage. Her
biggest strength however is that she will outclass all Earth Magic users 
until the end of the game (she'll tie one, but beats her out in other areas)

Weaknesses: Aila's weaknesses can be summed up easily: She runs out of gas 
later on. Although she'll be a great healer early on, once Joker gets his 
rune slot and Water Magic, she'll have little use as a healer. Although Aila 
can do some decent damage, she'll lose out to everyone except Jacques in a 
physical damage build, not to mention she requires a Fury Rune to even be a 
serious contender as a physical damager. Aila's also got pathetic defense and 
HP; she'll die easily if she's not protected. Thirdly, Aila has no Sharpshoot,
which makes her the only archer without a critical hit mark.

Name: Jeane
Automatic: No. Offer her a job at Budehec. She is in Brass Castle’s Rune 
Shop. 
Battle Fighting Style: Support
War Fighting Style: Support
Skills: Healing, Rune Sage

Jeane is the sexy Rune Sage in Brass Castle. No one knows much about her. 
It is suspected that she has a True Rune, but no one knows where it is. 
Jeane is at least 150 years old (very well put for someone that age, haha) 
She’s got white hair and wears a shiny black dress. 	
	
Support Discussion: Jeane, as always, is the rune sage. Her shop will sell any
commonly sold rune that is seen in a shop before, so if you need any runes, 
they can usually be found with her. Outside of the party, Jeane may attach or 
remove any non-permanent rune from any character, even if they are not in the 
active party. Inside the party, Jeane will heal a little damage, which is a 
nice perk, but truly shines in her Rune Sage ability, which causes rune 
chanting time to shrink, allowing for faster spells, not to mention allowing 
one to access a rune shop instantly. This ability is especially great if one 
is using the Pale Gate Rune, which has a naturally slower startuptime. Jeane's 
shop is located on the first screen of Budehec Castle, on the right end of 
the long building to the left of the stairs to the manor entrance.

Ernie is the young girl wearing the graduation robe and mortarboard in

useful skill nontheless. Ernie is located in the upstairs library, in the small

Hugo Chapter 1

Name: Hugo
Automatic: Yes
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Leader
Unique Skills: Parry, Heavy Damage, Counter-attack, Continual Attack

Hugo is the son of Lucia, the Karayan chief. He’s energetic, positive, 
and enjoys riding, whether it is on horses or on his pet griffon, Fubar. 
He’s selected to go visit Zexen, the empire to the west, and deliver an 
important message at the bequest of his mother. He’s got blonde hair that 
tapers to black in the tips, wears a bright yellow vest and black pants, and 
carries a long knife as a weapon.

Strengths: Hugo can really dish out pain fast, unbelievably fast. With a fast
weapon and high Swing, he'll get a lot of hits; with Heavy Damage, he'll really
dish it out, and with Continual Attack, he can keep on going. Defensively, Hugo
has an S in Repel and a respectable Parry that allows him to be nearly 
invincible to single targeted attacks. Hugo also gains access to a True Rune, 
and this allows him to really dish out some magical pain. 

Weaknesses: Despite Hugo's high defensive skills, magic or non-avoidable, 
non-parryable hits will show that Hugo has low defense and HP, and he can't 
avoid targeting like Queen does with Blue Ribbons since Hugo cannot equip them.
Also, Hugo doesn't have high magical marks, so, although he can do a fair 
amount of damage with his spells, he won't be casting them fast at all. This
is especially true if he is chosen to inherit the True Water Rune, since his 
Water Magic is the worst of his, Chris's, or Geddoe's.

Name: Sgt. Jordi (Joe)
Automatic: Yes, joins with Hugo 
Battle Fighting Style: Defensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Fighter
Unique Skills: Heavy Damage, Chant Volume 

Sgt. Joe is Hugo’s mentor. He’s a Duck village clansman and a veteran of 
wars. He is the voice of reason for the hotheaded and emotional boy, and the 
Sgt. constantly stops Hugo from giving into his anger. He wears a brightly 
colored helmet and vest, and uses a large halberd to attack. 

Strengths: Sgt. Joe dishes out pain much like Hugo does, with higher strength
and stronger weapons to take the place of many hits. Although Hugo will do more
damage, the Sgt. still has Heavy Damage and Damage enough to be formidable. 
Joe, since he is always with Hugo in the beginning, is easily the healer for 
his charge, with a better Water Magic score and starting with a Water Rune;
allowing him to handle that aspect that Hugo cannot provide very well. He's
also got the always-useful Magic Repel, which allows him to take no damage from
spells at times. 

Weaknesses: Sgt. Joe is not that great defensively, with defense only barely
better then Hugo without the Parry or great Repel. Especially in war battles, 
Joe tends to eat it much quicker then Hugo does. Also, Sgt. Joe can barely 
muster a second hit with his weapon, although he has an extra rune slot over
Hugo to help the gap, he will lose out in damage compared to those with more 
hits.

Name: Fubar
Automatic: Yes, with Hugo
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Fighter
Unique Skills: Freeze

Fubar is Hugo’s pet griffon, a cross between lion and bird. He’s very 
intelligent and can understand what Hugo tells him. He’s also very obedient, 
although he tends to go looking for food when he’s alone. Fubar has all white 
feathers except on the top of his wings, which are black. 

Strengths: Fubar can be divided into two sections: Mounted with Hugo and going
it solo. Mounted, Fubar is a powerful war machine, allowing Hugo to do more
damage (at the cost of the unit being considered as one, so if one dies, so
does the other). Solo, Fubar is definitely worse off. Regardless, Fubar still
has high damage and can stun enemies, so he's useful for that.

Weaknesses: Fubar is a monster, and thus, he can't equip any armor or 
acessories other then medicines or Sacrifical Jizos. So he can't regenerate
HP, gain resistance to spells, et cetera. His Shining Wing rune can be useful,
especially in war battles, since it doesn't unbalance and hits a fairly large
area, but for the most part, whenever Fubar is used, he should be on Hugo.

Name: Melville
Automatic: No. In Hugo Chapter 1, watch him argue with Guilliame. After that, 
enter the alleyway, climb up the scaffolding, and knock on the window, 
answering “Saint Loa Knights” when the voice talks. After the scene plays

recruit Billy, then wait to Chapter 4, and then talk to Melville in the 
alleyway where his clubhouse was. 
Battle Fighting Style: Offensive Fighter
War Fighting Style: Doesn’t Fight

Melville is the captain of the Saint Loa Knights, a group of 12 year olds who 
idolize the Zexen Knights and pretend to be knights themselves. Melville is 
the “Swordsman of Rage” of this group. He wears a white shirt, blue vest, 
brown highwater pants, has brown hair, and he uses a small sword to attack. 

Rating

Strengths: Melville has a surprisingly high Heavy Damage. When coupled with his
Swing and two rune slots, he can actually do damage that is surprising. He's 
decently fast too.

Weaknesses: Melville requires rare equipment to become a high damage dealer,
plus a lot of time since A+ skills like his Heavy Damage take longer to level
up then even S class skills. Also, Melville is very fragile and has a 
durability like that of mages, yet he doesn't have the protection of hanging
around back and casting spells. He could do that, did he not have a low MGC.
Oh well, he could do it with Water since that uses low level spells.

Nei is the young elf woman in the traveling troupe of music makers that

the group’s finances. She has blue hair and wears a white shirt and checkered 
pants. She uses her fists to attack. 

Strengths: Nei recieves a lot of praise among Suikoden players, and the reason
is her magic. Nei has a decent MGC score and high Water and Earth magic to 
make her a talented support mage, on the level of Salome. Nei's offense, 
because of her multiple hits, will be better then Salome's, as well as her 
magical defense. Nei's support advantage over Salome is her ability to use the 
Jongleur Rune, which allows Nei to chant and give her allies abilities as long

lots of damage, speed their chanting without using Jeane,or healing themselves

Weaknesses: If Nei is using the Jongleur, then she can't do anything else 
unless she stops, negating the effects. This really detracts from her magic.
The Jongleur too, although nice to do lots of damage, is really nothing more
then novelty considering the low HP of enemies and bosses in this game. The
1st level spell of Jongleur, that speeds chanting, is easily beaten by taking
Jeane into support, and the 4th level spell, which is healing, would be better
off having Nei take advantage of her A in Water. Lastly, Nei's physical damage
will only be decent if she sacrifices her rune slots, and thus, magic, for it.

the game from the eyes of the "enemy". He has borne the True Wind Rune his 
entire life, and now, seeks to destroy it to destroy a bleak, lifeless future 
that the True Wind Rune showed him. Luc is similar in appearence to Sasarai: 
he's short, has dark colored hair, and wears a green robe and cape. He uses a 
magic ring, exactly like Sasarai's, to attack. 

Strengths: Picture Estella, with her high magical attack. Now think of 
someone better. Luc possesses the magical offense like Estella does, only
he can do it better, with his permanent True Wind Rune, high marks all
around in magic, and a higher Focus so he doesn't lose his chanting. Couple
that with actual dodging ability, and he's nearly the perfect mage. 

Weaknesses: Luc, like most mages, has pathetic attack and defense.

Dressed in a black top hat and all black clothes, the blonde haired seeker of 
chaos is present in every Suikoden to date, but never has his role been
explained in such detail (even as vague as it is here). Yuber travels with 
Luc and Sarah to witness the destruction of the True Rune. Yuber himself is a 
True Rune bearer (his is called the Eightfold Rune), but he hates the True 
Runes immensely and longs for their destruction for the chaos they would 
cause. 

Strengths: Yuber has high offense. Very high offense. With a high Swing and

throw in his Eight-Devil Rune, which doubles his attack without penalty like
unbalance, the fact that he can use Lightning Magic almost as good as most
mages, and his great defensive abilities, and this guy is just sick...

Weaknesses: You can't use him in the main game, like all of Luc's Chapter

Hugo Chapter 2 – Battle at the Ancient Highway

Boss Hit Points
  Man in Black – 2,500

Recommended Formation
  Lilly
  Sgt. Joe
  Hugo
  Fubar
  Reed
  Samus

This battle can be considered one of the hardest battles, as the Man in Black 
has a very high evasion rate, and your characters here are mostly physical in 
nature. Lilly should start with the Tinto unite, while Hugo’s team attacks. 
The reason for the Tinto unite is not only just to give Lilly the Berserk 
status, but also to keep the three of them initially out of the battle. If 
you can help it, you want Hugo to be the target, as he and Fubar can take the 
most damage. Next turn, have Sgt. Joe use Kindness Drops on Hugo if he needs 
it, but if he doesn’t, Lilly should charge her sword with Sword of Rage to do 
more damage. 

The Man in Black will only attack or cast a weak Soaring Bolt, but he can do 
some serious damage physically. Samus can use his Wind rune to heal individual
characters if they survive his attacks. An important thing to note is who the 
Man in Black faces at the beginning of the round. That person will be the 
person the Man in Black's target. That pair of characters should defend 
rather then attack, as not only does that character take less damage, but 
also they have an increased chance to dodge his attacks. If they dodge, then 
the combo strings of the Man in Black will be stopped (Lilly may also Parry). 
Since the Man in Black never heals himself, just whittle him down until he 
dies. Equipping Sacrificial Jizos is a good idea. 


Chris Chapter 2 – Strategy Battle, Zexen vs. Lizard and Karaya Clan, Take Two.
 
This strategy battle takes place at the Great Hollow, and it’s payback for 
what the Lizard and Karaya Clans did in Chris Chapter 1. The goal is to kill 
5 units of Lizards and/or Karayans without losing Chris’s unit. Of course, 
since Chris is one hard woman to kill, it won’t be that hard. Unit 1 is Chris’s
unit; Roland leads Unit 2, and Leo heads up Unit 3. The first units that are 
your enemies are Lizard Clan units. The three lizard leaders lead the first 
three: Dupa, Shiba, and Bazba, while there are some other lizards led by 
generic Lizard Clan warriors. After a few turns, the Karayans will appear. 
The two named units are Lucia and Beechum, but some generic Karayan units 
also appear. Lucia is the head strategist of this army, as she has the 
Control of this army (at 5 units a turn), so wipe her out first. Once she is 
gone, your enemies will lose their strategist and can’t move as many units. 
Then pick off your enemies at your leisure. During the battle, you will gain 
four new units. One is led by Borus, another by Percival, and two are generic 
Zexen commander units, one with each commander. Borus appears to the north, 
and Percival to the southeast. Use them to ambush the Karayans and gain 
Cover. Salome, your strategist, will allow 6 units moved in a turn, which is 
more then enough. This battle is not too difficult, just designed to orient 
someone to the fighting style and gain a victory.

Flame Champion does not need Lvl 1 spells in the final battle, and even Chris 
can cast them quickly enough to take out the guardians without taking a hit. 


Chapter 5 – Incarnation of Wind Rune

Boss Hit Points
  WindRIncar – 11,000
  WaterRune – 4,000
  EarthRune – 3,500
  FireRune – 3,000
  LightningRune – 3,000

Alright, this is the final battle. Equip all high-level medicines, high-level
armor, and train up skills, especially Magic Resistance. If the character
doesn’t reach a high magic resistance or have the good stat, don’t bother
taking them; they’ll just get in the way 

Recommended Party – Here’s what a final party should support...

Flame Champion

Great Healer with Flowing or Shield Rune (Good Candidates: Percival, Salome, 
Beechum, Lucia, Joker, Aila)

Lightning Wizard with Thunder Rune (Good Candidates: Ace, Franz, Futch, Edge, 
Elaine)

Someone with decent Wind Magic and a Cyclone Rune for Shining Wind (Good 
Candidates: Queen, Yuiri, Reed, Roland)

Strong character with Heavy Damage (or something equivalent that can attack 
and dish out physical damage,. Also, make sure they have a high MDEF (Good 
Candidates: Lilly, Queen and Yuiri if you don’t use them in spot 4, Jacques 
and Yumi with Sharpshoot)

Backup Healer who can fight (Other healers I’ve mentioned) 

Support: Jeane – This is a magical battle, Rune Sage will be the most helpful. 

Arrangement with some of those examples
  Geddoe
  Percival
  Lilly
  Franz
  Lucia
  Queen

If the Flame Champion is Geddoe, be careful. He has the ability to really 
dish out the hurt with Hellfire, but you run the risk of the others getting 
hit. Stick with Final Flame on the Water Crystal. Franz should cast Thunder 
Storm, while Lucia targets the Lightning Crystal. 

The crystals cast spells like the True Runes do. The Water Crystal casts 
Mother Ocean and Heavenly Drops; the Lightning casts Furious Blow, Thunder 
Storm, and Hammer of Raijin; The Fire Crystal casts Final Flame and Hellfire. 
The Earth Crystal is unique, as it casts Earthquake, but it also casts a 
protective spell that surrounds your enemies with pylons and imposes a 
picture of the True Earth Rune. When this happens, don’t cast any offensive 
magic, as the spell will be absorbed and reflected back on your party for 
massive damage. 

Take out the Water Crystal first, as once it’s gone, the thing can’t cast 
Mother Ocean and the crystals, when destroyed, will stay dead. You’ll have to 
hack your way through the Lightning Crystal for your melee parties to reach 
it. Use Final Flame for your Flame Champion (If Lucia has a Rage Rune, use 
that first turn). 

The second turn, if the protective spell was not cast, follow these 
instructions. Lucia should heal with Kindness Rain, or, if you’ve bulked up 
her Wind Magic skill, Queen might be able to get away with Shining Wind to 
heal and attack. (It needs to be high though…) Franz should cast Furious Blow 
on the Water Crystal, while Geddoe’s uses a Final Flame. The Water Crystal 
should collapse, and the Lightning probably will as well. You’ve got some 
breathing room. Take out the Fire Crystal next, as Hellfire can seriously 
hurt. Once it’s gone, the Earth Crystal awaits. Percival and Lucia still heal 
with Kindness Rain, Percy as the main healer. 

If the protective spell is cast, the only spells you cast are healing spells 
(minus Shining Wind). Stay on physical offense. Use items if you run out of 
healing, but you shouldn’t need them. If you cast Guardian Earth, the 
protection spell will be removed without effect, it's the only safe way to
remove it. 
	
Note: You can use the other True Rune bearers in this battle. I don't like 
to, and I don't recommend it, it takes all the fun out of this battle. 
However, you can if you like, or if you're having a real hard time of the 
battle. 

Storyline Battles

Geddoe-

Geddoe's team was built for the final battle, as they play well off of each 
other and between themselves can accomplish a lot. Although Hugo's team could 
be considered a bit easier, that's because Hugo only needs three characters to
accomplish this, and two are a mounted pair. 

There are two ways to do this battle: The short, riskier way, and the longer, 
safer way

Short way formation

Geddoe
Queen
Ace
Joker
Jacques
Aila
(Jefferson)

Short way: Attach Violence Runes to everyone minus Aila, and an additional 
Double-Strike Rune on Geddoe and Joker. Start by attacking the Lightning 
crystal, with the archers focused on the Rincar. The Lightning Crystal should 
not be able to take all of that, and it will be destroyed. The next turn, use 
Mercenary B on the RIncar if you have Violence status. Violence status occurs 
when any character has lost half health. Merc B with all characters Violenced 
should do an obsence amount of damage. Combined with the attack on the RIncar 
that the archers (and any one who attacked after the LightningCrystal was 
destroyed) should do it in. If not, attack next turn, focus on the RIncar

Long Way formation
Geddoe 
Jacques
Ace
Joker
Queen
Aila
(Jeane)

Long Way: A magical battle is here. Geddoe should cast Hellfire, Ace should 
cast Thunder Storm, and Queen should cast The Shredding. Next turn, have Geddoe 
and Joker pair up with Final Flames on the Water Crystal, while Aila either 
heals with Great Blessing (if needed), or have her cast Canopy Defense for 
shielding. Once the Water Crystal is destroyed, go for the Lightning Crystal, 
then the Fire. Once those three are gone, it's all a matter of attacking and 
outlasting. Joker should be trained in Water Magic and Fire Magic both, and 
should have a pair of Rage and Flowing Runes. Flowing is for Kindness Rain 
only, Aila is on secondary healing; she heals until Joker finishes his Final 
Flames. 

Chris-

Formation

Roland
Leo

Percival
(Louis)

Chris's team is probably the most difficult, espeically if Louis is in support.
If one has high quality medicines equipped, Silent Lake should render the 
crystals useless and the RIncar only able to attack with one weaker attack. 
First turn Roland should cast The Shredding, Salome casts Battle Oath, and 
Percival lays down the Silent Lake. Bulk up Percy's Water Magic skill so it 
moves quickly. After that, it's an attacking game; take out the Lightning 
crystal to reach the RIncar, and the Water Crystal to prevent revival once 
Silent Lake wears off. After that, it's like Geddoe's team, only Salome is the
lead healer and Percy is on backup

Hugo-

always attack. Lilly is on offense while Sgt. Joe is the healer. Reed should
have a Cyclone Rune to cast Shining Wind, if needed. Samus can go any which 
way, but a Mother Earth Rune for Canopy Defense should help (just make sure to
