           Suikoden 3: Rare Armor
           This is a list of the rarest armor in Suikoden 3. Naturally, most of it won't be available until the later parts of the game.
           Posted by Blue Moon and Kuromimi on December 05, 2002

                   Armor Sets
                   When a character equips a complete Armor Set, it provides some kind of bonus (when the character is in your party). The sets that multiply potch won stacks just like the Potch
                   Finder Skill.

                   Destiny Set: Crown of Destiny, Robe of Destiny, Gloves of Destiny
                     Potch won after battle multiplied by 3.
                   Guardian Set: Guardian Casque, Guardian Chain Mail, Guardian Shield
                     Counter rate +50%.
                   Mole Set: Mole Helm, Mole Armor, Mole Shield, Mole Gloves
                     Make squeaky noises when you walk (not in battle).
                   Pale Moon Set: Pale Moon Casque, Pale Moon Leather, Pale Moon Necklace
                     Heal 25% of damage dealt after each standard attack.
                   Prosperity Set: Prosperity Hat, Prosperity Tunic, Prosperity Ring
                     Potch won after battle multiplied by 7.

               Elemental Resistance is determined by the greatest level of resistance worn by the character. For example, a character with Flame Casque (Level 2 Flame resistance) and a Flame
               Amulet (Amulets have Level 1 resistance) will have Level 2 Flame resistance. Elemental resistance will reduce the amount of damage taken by elemental spells that corresponding to
               that element by the following amounts...
                 Level 1 resistance: 20%
                 Level 2 resistance: 30%
                 Level 3 resistance: 40%
